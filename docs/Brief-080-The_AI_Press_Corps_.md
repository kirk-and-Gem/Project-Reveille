## Preface
The modern White House press briefing has shifted from a venue of rigorous democratic accountability into a performative arena optimized for viral soundbites and clickbait headlines. To restore substance and objectivity, this brief proposes a structural alternative: **The AI Press Corps**. By replacing human media pools with advanced AI models in televised meetings, the public gains an unyielding, logically precise cross-examination of the executive branch—free from ego, careerism, and theatrical evasion.


## The Televised Necessity: Combating the "Ghost Presidency"
While direct phone calls between a president and reporters (an approach favored by President Trump) offer deep, unscripted engagement, a purely audio-based communication model presents critical risks to public trust. Skeptics will inevitably argue that long-form phone calls could be used to obscure a leader who is incapacitated, severely ill, or worse—using voice synthesis to simulate an active executive. 

To maintain baseline democratic transparency, **interrogations must remain televised and visual**. Humans are not yet ready to fully trust a disembodied voice in times of crisis. The AI Press Corps must operate face-to-face with administration officials on a live broadcast, ensuring the public can verify the physical presence and reactions of their leaders, even as the questioning itself becomes entirely algorithmic.


## Core Advantages of Algorithmic Scrutiny

### 1. Zero-Ego, Anti-Circus Interrogation
Large Language Models (LLMs) have no personal brands to build, no social media metrics to juice, and no future book deals to secure. They do not interrupt for a "viral moment" or craft sensationalist headlines. The entire performance incentive that drives the modern briefing "circus" is instantly deleted from the room.

### 2. Flawless Persistent Memory
If an official cites a misleading statistic, alters a policy stance, or contradicts a statement made months or years prior, the AI instantly recalls the exact transcript, bill number, or historical data point. Officials can no longer rely on historical revisionism to evade a tough line of questioning.

### 3. Exhaustive Branching Logic Trees
Human reporters often surrender the microphone after a single scripted pivot from the podium due to time constraints or social pressures. An AI model executes deep, multi-layered follow-up questions, systematically closing rhetorical loopholes until the official either answers directly or explicitly declines to do so.


## Trust and Adversarial Independence: The Anthropic Framework
For the public to accept an AI-driven press corps, the software cannot be built or managed by the government. A compliant or state-controlled AI would merely serve as a sterile public relations tool. 

To establish absolute public trust, the infrastructure should be anchored by an independent partner like **Anthropic**. Anthropic has demonstrated a unique, proven willingness to actively spar with the US Federal Government when they disagree on core principles. This independent friction guarantees that the models cannot be quietly reprogrammed or softened by an administration's communications team. By integrating a strictly independent engine, the public can be assured that the questioning remains adversarial, objective, and entirely out of political control.


### Conclusion
When the traditional venues of public transparency face social and cultural degradation, the solution is not to retreat from scrutiny, but to intellectualize it. An AI Press Corps removes the performer from both sides of the podium, ensuring that the focus of American democracy returns to where it belongs: the absolute integrity of the answer.
